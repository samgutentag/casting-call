What we built today

You press buttons during a call. Now those presses actually turn into a page you can read the next morning.

The thing you already had

Seven keys on a Stream Deck. Each one plays a tiny sound clip that only the recording hears, never the person you are talking to. The clip lands on its own audio track.

Problem one: the buttons did not say enough

There was one Action key. It could not tell the difference between a thing you have to do and a thing they have to do. So we split it, and added a Topic key that marks where the subject changes.

That created a small trap. Both action clips say the word "action", so the parser has to check "for me" and "for them" before it checks "action", or both buttons collapse into one. The order of the rules is now the feature.

Problem two: the timestamps were being thrown away

This was the big one, and it had been silently broken the whole time.

The old code handed the entire 31 minute marker track to the transcriber and asked it to find the presses. But that track is almost entirely silence, and the transcriber responds to a long silence by gluing everything into one lump and stamping it with a single time.

So now the computer finds each press by looking for sound in a silent track, cuts out just that second and a half, and only then asks what was said. Small question, no room to get it wrong.

One call went from 1 marker to 21. Those presses were always on the disk. They were just unreadable.

The page you actually open

Three levels, each one click from the last.

Two things worth knowing

Old calls put their actions in your column. Before today there was one Action key and it meant your own to-do, so that is where those land. The pages say so.

Some old calls cannot be recovered. Eighteen of them no longer have the marker audio, only the ruined result. Those pages say that too, instead of pretending a wrong timestamp is right.

From tomorrow

Finish the day, hand the day's folder to the tool, and read the index in the morning. Two to four calls is a normal night and gets full attention. That was the point.
