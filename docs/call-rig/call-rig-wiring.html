Set once, then leave alone

  1. On the P4next, set Recorder Mode to Multi Track, set USB Audio Interface to Stereo Mix, set the PLAY key during recording to Mark Only, and put a short beep on pad A in One-Shot mode.
  2. In macOS and Meet, choose ZOOM P4next for both speaker and mic.
  3. In OBS, replace the Wave source with a ZOOM P4next audio input on track 1 only, and keep a macOS audio capture (desktop or app audio) on track 2 only. Turn monitoring off for both.
  4. Set the P4next's date and time, so its files can be matched to OBS by start time if the audio match ever fails.
  5. Mute inputs 2 to 4 on the P4next (MUTE 2–4 lit) so the empty jacks don't add noise. Leave MUTE 1 off.

Hearing yourself

  1. The P4next's headphone jacks always carry the full mix, your mic included. The manual lists no way to take one input out of the headphones.
  2. That monitoring happens inside the P4next and never goes through the Mac, so there's no software round trip. The delay you've felt comes from monitoring in Wave Link or OBS.
  3. To keep yourself out of your ears, turn OBS monitoring off and close Wave Link during calls. If the P4next's own monitoring still feels off, lower the headphone volume rather than adding software.

Test on the first call: confirm that the far side hears you, that they don't hear the marker beep, and that OBS's single track has your voice. The manual says the USB stereo mix carries inputs 1 to 4; whether the sound pads are left out of it isn't stated.
