Call Rig Wiring

OBS is the recording: the P4next sends every channel to the Mac over one USB-C cable, so OBS gets your mic, the call and the marker on their own tracks with no app audio capture in the way. The P4next also writes every channel to its microSD card, the backup that fills any gap OBS leaves.

Set once, then leave alone

  1. On the P4next: recording file type Multi Track, USB Audio Interface Mode Multi Track, Mix Minus On, Play Key in Rec Mark Only, pad A = MARKER.WAV in One-Shot.
  2. In macOS and Meet, choose ZOOM P4next for both speaker and mic.
  3. In OBS, scene Call P4: three Audio Input Capture sources on ZOOM P4next, downmixing off. P4 Mic Ch 1 / Ch 1 on tracks 1 and 2, P4 Computer Ch 7 / Ch 8 on tracks 1 and 3, P4 Marker Ch 9 / Ch 10 on track 4 only. Monitoring off, Global Audio Devices disabled.
  4. Set the P4next's date and time, so its files can be matched to OBS by start time if no marker press is found.
  5. Mute inputs 2 to 4 on the P4next (MUTE 2–4 lit) so the empty jacks don't add noise. Leave MUTE 1 off.

Hearing yourself

  1. The P4next's headphone jacks always carry the full mix, your mic included. The manual lists no way to take one input out of the headphones.
  2. That monitoring happens inside the P4next and never goes through the Mac, so there's no software round trip.
  3. Keep OBS monitoring off on all three sources. If the P4next's own monitoring still feels off, lower the headphone volume rather than adding software.

Tested 2026-11-14: the far side hears you and not the marker, each OBS track carries only its channel, and the card's mix has both sides. With Mix Minus on, the P4next's own USB mix (channels 11-12) leaves out the call, which is why OBS builds track 1 itself.
